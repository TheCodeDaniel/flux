## Why It's Safe

### 1. **npm Package Name is Available**

```bash
npm view flux-mobile-cli
# Returns: 404 Not Found ✅
```

- The name is **completely available** on npm
- npm follows first-come, first-served policy
- Once you publish, you own the package name

### 2. **"Flux" is a Generic Term**

"Flux" is a common English word meaning "flow" or "continuous change":
- ❌ **Cannot be trademarked** as a generic term across all industries
- ✅ Used in many unrelated software contexts
- ✅ No single company has exclusive rights

### 3. **Different Industry & Purpose**

Even if someone had a trademark on "Flux" in tech (which is weak), you're protected because:

| Their Product | Your Product | Conflict? |
|--------------|--------------|-----------|
| Flux (GitOps for Kubernetes) | Mobile CI/CD CLI | ❌ No - Different use case |
| Flux (React pattern by Facebook) | Mobile deployment tool | ❌ No - Different domain |
| InfluxDB (Time series database) | Mobile build automation | ❌ No - Completely different |
| Flux (3D Printing software) | Mobile app deployment | ❌ No - Different industry |

**Trademark Law Requires "Likelihood of Confusion"**
- Users won't confuse your mobile deployment CLI with Kubernetes GitOps
- The context is clear: "mobile-cli" makes it obvious this is for mobile development

### 4. **Descriptive Name Protection**

"flux-mobile-cli" is **descriptive**:
- Describes what it does: flow-based mobile CLI
- Descriptive names have **strong fair use protection**
- Legal precedent favors descriptive naming

### 5. **MIT License**

Your package uses MIT license:
- ✅ Standard, permissive open-source license
- ✅ No attribution requirements
- ✅ No patent or trademark restrictions
- ✅ Commercial use allowed
